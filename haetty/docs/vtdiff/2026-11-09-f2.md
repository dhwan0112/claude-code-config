## What changed in haetty-vt

- **F2a, C0 controls 0x10–0x1F do nothing in ground state.** The newest Ghostty ignores them and cancels an escape
  on 0x18 and 0x1A (`src/terminal/stream.zig:1493`, e035beb). 0.2.2 printed them as cells, and haetty followed it.
  DESIGN section 10 updated.
- **F2b, C1 bytes.** From 0x80 in a DCS string the bytes are payload: the newest Ghostty does not honour 8-bit C1
  controls there (`src/terminal/parse_table.zig`, dcs_passthrough). A C1 control sent as UTF-8 (U+0080–U+009F) is
  ignored, not printed (`src/terminal/stream.zig`, "C1 controls decoded from UTF-8 are ignored"). A raw C1 byte
  still prints U+FFFD (invalid UTF-8, both references).
- **F2b, DECRQSS** (`DCS $ q Pt ST`) answered as the newest Ghostty (`src/terminal/dcs.zig`, `Terminal.printAttributes`):
  SGR (attributes in its order, then the colours), DECSTBM, DECSCUSR and DECSLRM (with margins on); `0` for anything
  else. VT510's DECRPSS. Checked on seven minimal inputs: all equal to the newest Ghostty.
